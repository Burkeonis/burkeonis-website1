ABOUT BURKEONIS

This didn't start as a brand. It started as a question I couldn't stop asking myself.

Why do I keep ending up back here? Why do the same patterns keep following me? Why does changing everything around me never seem to change me?

Eventually I realized something I spent years trying to avoid.

I carried myself into every new beginning.

MY STORY

I'm not here because I've lived a perfect life.

I've made mistakes. I've hurt people. I've been hurt by people. I've struggled with addiction. I've carried anger, shame, fear, regret, and more excuses than I care to admit.

For a long time I believed if I could just change my surroundings, everything would finally be different.

A new relationship. A new city. A new job. Another chance.

But every time I started over, I brought the same version of myself with me.

That's when I stopped trying to escape my life and started trying to understand it.

WHAT BURKEONIS IS

Burkeonis isn't an alter ego.

It isn't a character I play.

It isn't the fearless version of me.

Burkeonis is the name I gave the part of myself that finally stopped running.

The part willing to look honestly at my life instead of constantly looking for someone else to blame.

It reminds me that growth begins the moment excuses end.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Everything you see on this website comes from the same idea.

The music. The apps. The worksheets. The writing. The conversations.

They all exist for one reason.

To help people slow down long enough to see themselves clearly.

I don't want to tell people what to think.

I want to ask the questions most of us spend our lives avoiding.

Because I know what happens when we avoid them.

Nothing changes.

You cannot change what you're unwilling to look at.

WHAT I BELIEVE

Reflection comes before change.

Truth comes before comfort.

Ownership comes before growth.

Healing isn't pretending the past didn't happen.

Healing is refusing to let it decide the rest of your life.

IF YOU'RE STILL HERE...

Thank you.

Whether you came here because of the music, the apps, the writing, or simple curiosity, I appreciate that you took the time to look around.

I don't have all the answers.

I'm still learning. I'm still rebuilding. I'm still finding parts of myself I didn't know existed.

Burkeonis isn't a destination.

It's the work.

If you're willing to do that work too...

Welcome.
