THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

MY NAME IS
JEFFERY BURKE.

Burkeonis is what happened when I stopped trying to make my life look acceptable and started telling the truth about what it had actually become.

THIS IS NOT A SUCCESS STORY WRITTEN FROM THE FINISH LINE.It is work being built from inside the wreckage.
01

BEFORE THE NAME

I LEARNED HOW TO SURVIVE LONG BEFORE I LEARNED HOW TO LIVE.

I grew up carrying things I did not have words for. I learned early that pain was something you swallowed, weakness was something people used against you, and needing anyone was dangerous. Survival became a personality before I was old enough to know the difference.

I became good at reading rooms, watching faces, hearing changes in tone and preparing for whatever might come next. Some of that kept me alive. Some of it followed me into places where the danger was already gone.

02

THE PART PEOPLE PREFER TO SKIP

I HAVE HURT PEOPLE. I HAVE BEEN HURT. BOTH ARE TRUE.

Addiction, anger, trauma, mental health, broken relationships and years of living in fight-or-flight have shaped my life. They explain parts of me. They do not erase my responsibility for what I have done while trying to survive them.

I have run. I have blamed. I have reacted before I understood what I was reacting to. I have confused intensity with truth and survival with strength. I have also kept getting back up when it would have been easier to disappear inside the same cycle.

Understanding why I became this way matters. Using it as permission to stay this way does not.
03

THE LOSS UNDER EVERYTHING

I AM A FATHER LIVING WITH THE WEIGHT OF NOT BEING WHERE I SHOULD BE.

I have three sons. The distance between us is not a small detail in my story. It is one of the deepest wounds in it. There are days, years and moments I cannot recover. There are things I wish I had understood sooner and choices I would change if wanting it badly enough could change the past.

It cannot. So the question becomes harder: what kind of man do I build from here? That question sits underneath the music, the protocols, Self Mirror and nearly everything on this site.

04

WHY BURKEONIS EXISTS

I COULD NOT FIND TOOLS THAT SPOKE TO PEOPLE WHILE LIFE WAS STILL UGLY.

A lot of self-help is written after the crisis has been cleaned up. It uses calm language for people whose minds are not calm. It tells you to breathe when your body thinks it is under attack, to journal when you cannot organize one thought, and to love yourself when you do not even recognize who is standing in the mirror.

I needed something more direct. Something that could sit in the room with addiction, grief, shame, rage, attachment, misophonia, dissociation and relationship chaos without becoming clinical wallpaper or motivational bullshit.

Burkeonis is my attempt to build that. Not from authority. From proximity.

WHAT THE NAME MEANS

BURKEONIS IS NOT AN ALTER EGO.

It is the name I gave the part of me formed by hurt, survival, drugs, loss, anger, creativity and the refusal to let those things have the final word.

Jeffery lived it. Burkeonis found a way to say it. The work is bringing those two people back together instead of letting one hide behind the other.

A NECESSARY LINE

I AM NOT A DOCTOR, THERAPIST OR FINISHED PRODUCT.

I do not diagnose people and this site does not replace professional care. I share what I have lived, what I am learning, and tools built to help people slow a reaction down long enough to see what is underneath it.

Some days I am teaching from what I survived. Other days I am building the tool I need that same night. I will not pretend those are the same thing, and I will not pretend I am above the work I ask anyone else to do.

THE POINT

I AM NOT TRYING TO PROVE THAT PEOPLE CAN BECOME PERFECT.

I am trying to prove that a life can be confronted while it is still unfinished.

That accountability does not require self-destruction. That understanding your past does not mean handing it control of your future. That even when you have lost people, time, trust and versions of yourself you cannot recover, there is still a decision left to make.

You decide what gets built from what remains.
