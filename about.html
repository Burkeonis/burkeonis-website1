THE VERSION OF ME THAT SURVIVED

Burkeonis wasn’t created from inspiration — it was created from survival. From pressure, loss, fractured identity, and the decision to stop living as a version of myself built only to endure. This project is the result of choosing truth over performance.

WHERE THIS STARTED

For years, I lived in a state of constant reaction — protecting myself, hiding parts of who I was, and carrying weight that wasn’t mine. The music, the tools, and the entire Burkeonis world came from the moment I realized I couldn’t keep living like that.

I didn’t build this to impress anyone. I built it because I needed a way to understand myself without distortion. And once I did, I realized other people needed the same thing.

WHO I AM NOW

I am not the person I used to be. I am not the version of myself that stayed quiet, stayed small, or stayed contained. Burkeonis is the version of me that survived — the one that decided to stop pretending everything was fine.

Everything you see here — the music, the apps, the tools, the writing — comes from that version. The one that finally chose honesty.

WHY I BUILT THIS

I built Burkeonis because I wanted to create something real. Something that didn’t hide behind aesthetics or performance. Something that could help people understand themselves the way I had to learn to understand myself — through truth, confrontation, and clarity.

This world exists so you don’t have to carry everything alone.
