THE BURKEONIS APPS

These tools were built from the moments when clarity mattered most. They are not meant to entertain — they are meant to reveal. Each one is designed to help you see yourself without distortion.

SELF MIRROR

A direct reflection of your internal state. No filters, no softening, no avoidance. The Self Mirror shows you what you are actually carrying, not what you pretend to carry. It is built for honesty, not comfort.

RAD MIRROR

The RAD Mirror is built for people who react fast, feel deep, and struggle to explain why. It breaks down your reactions into patterns, triggers, and emotional signatures — giving you a map of what is actually happening beneath the surface.

OPEN RAD MIRROR →

WHY THESE EXIST

These apps were created during the hardest parts of the Burkeonis journey — when everything felt chaotic, overwhelming, and impossible to organize. They became the tools that made sense of the noise.

They are not meant to fix you. They are meant to help you understand yourself well enough to stop repeating the same cycles.
