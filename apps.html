THE APPS CAME FROM REAL PROBLEMS.

I did not build these because the world needed more apps. I built them because I kept running into moments where I needed something that could slow me down, cut through the noise, and show me what I was missing.

They are not here to diagnose you, fix your life, or tell you who you are. They are here to help you look more honestly at what is already happening.

BURKEONIS ATLAS

Atlas is the central map of the Burkeonis ecosystem. It brings the work, tools, protocols, music, and personal resources into one place so people can find what they need without getting lost between projects.

It is built to organize the weight you are carrying, show where you are, and give you a clearer route toward the next useful action.

Atlas does not pretend life is clean or linear. It gives the chaos a structure without stripping away the truth that made Burkeonis necessary.

SELF MIRROR

Self Mirror is built to reflect the gap between what you say, what you do, and what you keep avoiding.

It is not there to agree with you. It asks questions, points out patterns, and challenges contradictions when your words and actions stop matching.

You bring the truth you are willing to tell. Self Mirror helps you notice the parts you may still be leaving out.

OPEN SELF MIRROR →

RAD MIRROR

RAD Mirror was built for the moments when a reaction becomes bigger than the conversation that started it.

It helps break down what happened, what was triggered, what you felt, what you did, and what might have been happening underneath the anger, panic, shutdown, or need to protect yourself.

It does not excuse harmful behaviour. It helps you understand the reaction clearly enough to take responsibility for what comes next.

TATTOO FORGE

Tattoo Forge is being built for people who want more than a random image pulled from a trend.

It will combine personal meaning, symbols, runes, mythology, astrology, numerology, placement, and tattoo style into one design process.

The goal is not to pretend a symbol has ancient meaning when it does not. The goal is to create something original, explain what it represents, and give the person wearing it a design that actually belongs to them.

WHY THESE EXIST

I know what it feels like to react before thinking, repeat the same patterns, and understand what happened only after the damage is done.

Awareness does not automatically change anything. You can understand yourself and still keep doing the same thing.

These tools are meant to create a pause. A place between the reaction and the next decision.

They are not replacements for therapy, medical care, or real human support. They are tools for reflection.

Because you cannot change what you refuse to look at.
