FIELD NOTES / THE METHOD

STOP COLLECTING ADVICE. START BUILDING CASE FILES.

SEPTEMBER 2026JEFFERY BURKE~5 MIN READ

Most advice sounds intelligent when nothing is happening. Set boundaries. Trust yourself. Communicate. Let go. The problem is not that these ideas are wrong. The problem is that they are too vague to survive contact with the moment you actually need them.

A case file does something different. It makes you slow down long enough to separate the event from the story, the pattern from the excuse, and the feeling from the decision. It gives your future self a record that your frightened, angry, exhausted, or hopeful self cannot quietly rewrite.

You do not need more slogans. You need evidence you can return to.

START WITH WHAT A CAMERA COULD PROVE

Record the date, the event, the words used, the action taken, and what happened next. Interpretation comes later. This creates a stable floor. Without it, every new mood can produce a new version of the past.

TRACK THE REPEAT, NOT JUST THE CRISIS

One missed agreement might be an accident. Ten missed agreements with ten convincing explanations are a pattern. The same is true for your own behaviour. A case file removes the comfort of treating every repeat as a completely new situation.

For each episode, record the trigger, body state, first thought, protective move, immediate payoff, and delayed cost. Over time, the sequence becomes visible. Once you can predict the sequence, you can choose where to interrupt it.

END WITH A DECISION THAT CAN BE OBSERVED

Insight without action becomes another way to avoid the decision. Finish with one move, an owner, and a time. Make the evidence for completion obvious. Then schedule the review. The point is not to produce beautiful pages. The point is to stop having the same realization without changing the next event.

USE THE RIGHT FILE FOR THE RIGHT PATTERN

Relationships, identity, addiction, grief, burnout, money, leadership, parenting, perfectionism, and self-sabotage overlap, but they are not interchangeable. Each needs different questions. A complete system lets you follow the evidence instead of forcing every problem through the same explanation.
