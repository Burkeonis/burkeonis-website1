WHY I'M WRITING THIS DOWN

AUGUST 2026JEFFERY BURKE~4 MIN READ

I built the tools first. Self Mirror, the protocols, the music, No Last Words — all of it went up before there was anywhere to just talk. That order was backwards, and I know it. It's easier to hand someone a finished thing than to say, in your own words, why you built it and what it's costing you to keep building it. This is me fixing that.

So here's the honest version of what this blog is: it's not a content calendar, and I'm not going to pretend there's a team behind this account. There isn't. When something goes up here, I wrote it, usually late, usually because something happened that day worth writing down instead of just carrying around.

I made a lot of mistakes, but there has not been one day when I wasn't trying to find my way home.

That's the line underneath everything on this site, and it's the line underneath this blog too. Not as a slogan — I don't do those — but as the actual reason any of this exists. Self Mirror exists because I needed a way to catch myself lying to myself. The protocols exist because vague advice is useless when you're actually in it. No Last Words exists because some conversations end before you're ready, and I know exactly what that does to a person.

WHAT'S ACTUALLY GOING TO BE HERE

Three kinds of posts, and I'm not going to dress them up as more than they are:

What I'm building. New tools, changes to old ones, why I made a call one way instead of another. Including the calls that didn't work.

What I'm working through. Fatherhood, distance from my kids, addiction, the days the work is easy and the days it isn't. Not a highlight reel. I'm not interested in performing recovery for an audience — I'm interested in being useful to people going through something similar.

What I'm noticing. Patterns, questions, things people send me that changed how I think about a tool or a protocol. If you email me something that reshapes a post, I'll say so.

What won't be here: manufactured inspiration, a finish-line I haven't crossed, or advice I haven't actually tested on myself first. If a post reads like it was written by someone who has this figured out, tell me, because that's not who's writing it.

HOW TO ACTUALLY REACH ME

hello@burkeonis.com goes to me. Not a support queue, not a filtered inbox — me. If something here lands wrong, or lands right, or you've got a question about a tool, or you just want to say what happened when you used one, that's the address. I read it. I can't promise I'll answer everything fast, but I'm not going to pretend I don't see it either.

That's the whole pitch, because there isn't one. I'm writing this down because the tools were never supposed to be the whole story — they were supposed to be proof that the story could go somewhere different. This is the part where I say what that's actually looking like, one post at a time.
