THE BOOK / WRITING IN PROGRESS

THIS IS NOT A CLEAN
REDEMPTION STORY.

It is the longer version of everything I could not fit into a song. A lived account of addiction, loss, broken relationships, fractured identity, survival, creativity, and the consequences of spending years running from the truth.

Part memoir, part field record, and part confrontation, the Burkeonis book examines how pain becomes a pattern, how survival strategies become cages, and what happens when the person who endured the damage must also take responsibility for the damage they caused.

This is not a book written by someone pretending to have life solved. It does not offer easy answers, manufactured inspiration, or a polished version of recovery. It follows the unfinished work of facing the past without allowing it to control the future.

At its heart, this is a book about breaking cycles: recognizing the identities we built to survive, understanding why we keep returning to the same wounds, and choosing to stop abandoning ourselves when the truth becomes uncomfortable.

THE STORY IS STILL BEING WRITTEN BECAUSE THE WORK IS STILL BEING LIVED.
