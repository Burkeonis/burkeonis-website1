THE MISSION PAIN INTO PURPOSE

WHY BURKEONIS EXISTS

I HAVE BEEN
THROUGH HELL.
I AM STILL HERE.

Burkeonis exists to turn lived experience into something that can help another person find their footing.

I know what it is like when addiction, loss, broken relationships, fractured identity, and everything else hit at the same time. I am not building from a distance. I am building from what I survived.

The mission is simple: make honest music, practical tools, and reflective work that help people understand themselves without confusing, judging, or pretending to fix them.

I cannot choose anyone’s path for them. I can hold up a mirror, share what I learned, and create a safer place to pause before the next decision.

YOU ARE NOT WHAT HAPPENED TO YOU. THE TRUTH CAN BECOME A WAY FORWARD.
BEHIND BURKEONIS CREATOR / ARTIST / BUILDER

ABOUT BURKEONIS

THE VERSION
OF ME THAT
SURVIVED.

Burkeonis isn’t a character. It’s the version of me that survived.

This project was built from addiction, loss, fractured identity, broken relationships, and the decision to stop pretending everything was fine. Every song, every piece of art, every word, and every project starts with one question:

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU STOP LYING TO YOURSELF?

That’s what Burkeonis is about.

This isn’t music made to chase trends. It’s music for the people who live with chaos in their heads, who question everything, who carry scars nobody sees, and who are trying to become something stronger without pretending they were never broken.

Burkeonis is more than music.

It’s a growing collection of ideas, tools, stories, and experiences built around self-reflection, personal growth, creativity, and rebuilding your life from the ground up.

If something I create helps you face a truth you’ve been avoiding, then it has done its job.

I’m not here to tell you who to become.

I’m here to remind you to find out who you already are.
