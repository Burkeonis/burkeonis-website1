YOU CARRY YOURSELF EVERYWHERE YOU GO.

You can leave the place, the person, or the past.
You still bring yourself with you.

Burkeonis came from the parts of my life I could not keep avoiding. The anger. The grief. The addiction. The damage I caused. The things done to me. The person I became while trying to survive all of it.

The music says what I could not say properly. The apps and tools came from trying to understand my own reactions, patterns, contradictions, and the distance between who I said I was and how I was actually living.

This is not a finished story, a clean recovery arc, or a collection of easy answers. It is the work of looking directly at yourself and deciding what you are going to do with what you find.
