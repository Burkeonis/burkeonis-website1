THE INTERNAL ARCHIVE

The journal is where the internal world becomes visible. These entries capture the thoughts, realizations, and emotional shifts that shaped the Burkeonis project. Nothing here is polished — it is raw, honest, and written from the inside.

ENTRY 001 — THE MOMENT IT BROKE

2026

There was a moment where everything I was carrying became too heavy to hide. Not a dramatic collapse — just a quiet realization that pretending was killing me. That moment became the start of Burkeonis.

ENTRY 002 — THE SHIFT

2026

The shift didn’t happen all at once. It came in pieces — small decisions, small confrontations, small truths. Every one of them felt impossible until it wasn’t. That’s how change actually works.

ENTRY 003 — THE COST OF HONESTY

2026

Honesty has a cost. You lose versions of yourself. You lose people who only knew the mask. You lose the comfort of pretending. But you gain something real — a life that doesn’t require performance.
