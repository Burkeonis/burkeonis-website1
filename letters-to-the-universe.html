MASTERED RECORD / DEEP ANALYSIS

LETTERS TO
THE UNIVERSE.

This is one of the clearest windows into who I was when I wrote it.

Looking back now, I realize it is not really a song to the universe. It is a conversation between three versions of myself: the man I was, the shadow that remembers everything, and the man I am trying to become.

THE REAL CONVERSATION
The universe is not the one speaking. It acts as a witness. The real conversation is happening inside me.
01 / THE OPENING

TONIGHT I AM NOT HIDING.

The opening is not a cry for rescue. It is a declaration. I am not asking someone to save me; I am asking to finally be seen.

I do not tell my shadow to leave. I invite it to stand beside me. Most people spend their lives trying to outrun the parts of themselves they hate. This song begins with the opposite idea: I am finally willing to look.

02 / THE WRECKAGE

GRIEVING THE LIVES THAT NEVER HAPPENED.

This is not about one breakup or one mistake. It is about living inside alternate versions of my life—every almost, every missed opportunity, and every road not taken.

I was not only grieving what I had done. I was grieving what I never did: words I never said, love I never expressed, boundaries I never set, and dreams I never chased. Sometimes silence leaves deeper scars than failure.

Every night I chose my armor when I should have dropped my pride.

Armor protects. Armor also isolates. The thing I believed was keeping me safe was also keeping people away.

03 / SHADOW AND UNIVERSE

ONE REMEMBERS. ONE ASKS WHAT COMES NEXT.

The shadow is not evil. Its job is to remember. The universe does not erase the pain; it asks what I am going to do with it. One preserves the past. The other transforms it.

I carried guilt like stones in my coat. A coat is something you wear. Without realizing it, guilt had become more than an emotion. It had become a uniform—part of my identity.

04 / THE CHORUS

ENDING THE WAR—NOT DESTROYING THE SHADOW.

The chorus does not ask for forgiveness. It asks for peace. I am not saying I am done with my shadow. I am saying I am done fighting it.

Strength sometimes looks like falling apart.

Sometimes the strongest thing a person can do is stop pretending they are okay. Breaking can be the beginning of honesty.

Underneath the song is one fear: I do not want my mistakes to become my entire story. If something greater remembers my life, remember that I tried.

05 / BEHIND THE MASK

ANGER WAS FEAR. PRIDE WAS PROTECTION.

The song starts peeling away the mask. My shadow knows what I never say aloud: the fear behind the anger and the quiet behind the pride.

Regret is just love that missed its cue.

Regret exists because something mattered. It is not proof that I am broken. It is proof that I loved, even when I failed to show it.

Every mistake was a teacher with teeth. Pain taught me, but not gently. Every lesson bit first. Only afterward did it become wisdom.

06 / THE BRIDGE

THE SHADOW BECOMES PROOF OF SURVIVAL.

Instead of rejecting the shadow, I thank it. It is not evidence that I am damaged. It is evidence that I survived what damaged me.

When I ask the universe not to judge me, I am projecting my own judgment onto it. What I actually want is permission to stop punishing myself.

I spent years swimming upstream, trying to recover versions of myself that no longer existed. They cannot be recovered. They can only be remembered and carried differently.

07 / INTEGRATION

WALKING WITH MY SHADOW AS A FRIEND.

The final chorus changes the ghosts into lessons—not because the pain disappeared, but because its meaning changed. The goal is not destroying the shadow. The goal is understanding it.

The man I become will forgive the man I was.

I do not ask the universe, another person, or even my shadow to forgive me. I ask my future self. The forgiveness I wanted most was always my own.

08 / WHAT IT MEANS NOW

THE BLUEPRINT FOR BURKEONIS.

When I wrote this, I was taking inventory of my entire life. I was tired of carrying regret as if it were my identity. I wanted my past to become part of my story instead of becoming my prison.

I was not looking for excuses. I was looking for meaning. The question was: How do I carry everything that happened without letting it become who I am?

Today, the song feels like a blueprint for everything Burkeonis represents: the music, the website, Self Mirror, and the shadow work. My strongest work is not only about exposing wounds. It is about transforming them.

It is not a goodbye to my shadow.

It is not surrender.

IT IS RECONCILIATION.

The moment I stopped asking my darkness to disappear—and started asking it to walk beside me.
