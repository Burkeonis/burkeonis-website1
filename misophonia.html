BURKEONIS / SOUND RESPONSE SYSTEM

THE SOUND IS SMALL.
THE IMPACT IS NOT.

Misophonia can turn breathing, chewing, laughing, tapping, bass, plastic bags or one repeated sound into an internal emergency. The room may be safe while your nervous system is already preparing for war.

USE THE RAPID RESETMAP A TRIGGER
THIS IS NOT “BEING ANNOYED.”

The reaction can arrive before thought: heat, rage, panic, disgust, pressure, muscle tension, escape urgency or shutdown.

I built this because sound does not merely interrupt my world. Sometimes it takes the whole room hostage.

JEFFERY BURKE / BURKEONIS

WHEN THE TRIGGER IS HAPPENING NOW

THE 90-SECOND SOUND BREAK

01

BREAK CONTACT

Turn away, change rooms, cover one ear safely, use headphones or create distance. This is regulation, not defeat.

02

DROP THE VERDICT

For ninety seconds, stop deciding whether the sound is rude, deliberate or unfair. Name only the body reaction.

03

UNLOCK THE BODY

Release your jaw. Lower your shoulders. Open your hands. Exhale longer than you inhale four times.

04

RETURN WITH A PLAN

Choose one next move: remain with protection, ask for a change, leave temporarily or end the situation.

WHAT THIS SPACE IS, AND IS NOT.

This is a lived-experience regulation tool. It is not a diagnosis, cure, clinical treatment or replacement for professional care.

Misophonia is not permission to control, shame or harm other people. It is also not something you should be mocked into silently enduring. The work is protection without destruction.
