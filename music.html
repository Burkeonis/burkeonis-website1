LISTEN TO THE CAGE DOOR →

ABOUT THE SONG

The Cage Door was written from the feeling that something inside was trying to break out — fed up with the pain, fed up with the silence, fed up with being contained. This track is a purge, a release, and a confrontation with everything that has been carried too long.

WHAT IT MEANS

The cage is not only what happened — it is every version of self built to survive it: silence, rage, control, shame, and the belief that staying contained was safer than being seen. The door opens when the protection becomes the thing that suffocates.
