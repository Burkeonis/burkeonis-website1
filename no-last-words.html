THE REASON BEHIND THE SONG

GRIEF DOES NOT ALWAYS ARRIVE WITH AN ENDING.

No Last Words was written for sudden loss. The kind where life changes before your mind can catch up and the person is gone before the conversation is finished.

You carry the questions because there is nowhere left to put them. If I had known. If I had called. Why them. Why now. What would I have said. What did they know. Did they know I loved them.

Life keeps moving even when your world has stopped. That is not fairness. It is the brutal machinery of being alive. Healing does not mean leaving them behind. It means learning how to carry the love without letting the unanswered parts bury you beside it.
