Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 20, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information Self Mirror collects, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices you have regarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Self Mirror, we may collect:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Journal entries, reflections, and conversations you choose to save.
  • Subscription and billing status.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and usage logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain your account.
  • Generate AI-powered reflections and insights.
  • Save your journals and conversation history.
  • Process subscriptions and payments.
  • Improve platform performance and security.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.
  • Respond to customer support requests.

3. Payment Processing

Payments are securely processed through Stripe. Self Mirror does not store your complete credit card information. Payment details are handled according to Stripe's own privacy and security practices.

4. AI Processing

Content you submit may be processed by artificial intelligence in order to generate responses and reflections. AI-generated responses are created automatically and should not be considered professional advice.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information using modern security practices. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account.
  • Request removal of stored personal data where applicable.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time.

7. Cookies

Self Mirror may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, remember preferences, maintain login sessions, and analyze website performance.

8. Third-Party Services

Self Mirror may use trusted third-party providers including authentication services, hosting providers, analytics services, cloud infrastructure, and payment processors. These providers only receive information necessary to perform their services.

9. Children's Privacy

Self Mirror is not intended for children under the minimum legal age required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Self Mirror after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the official Burkeonis website.
