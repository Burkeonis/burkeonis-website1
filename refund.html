Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: July 20, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how subscription cancellations, refunds, and one-time purchases are handled for Self Mirror.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account or the payment provider. Cancellation prevents future renewals but does not automatically generate a refund for payments already processed.

2. Monthly Subscriptions

After cancellation, you will continue to have access to paid features until the end of your current billing period. Your subscription will then expire automatically.

3. Refund Requests

If you believe you were charged in error or experienced a technical issue that prevented access to the service, you may contact us to request a refund. Refund requests are reviewed individually and are not guaranteed.

4. Founder Lifetime Membership

Founder Lifetime memberships are one-time purchases that provide lifetime access to the included features while Self Mirror remains available. Because access is granted immediately, these purchases are generally non-refundable except where required by applicable law.

5. Payment Processing

Payments are securely processed by Stripe. Refunds approved by Self Mirror will be issued through Stripe using the original payment method whenever possible.

6. Fraud & Abuse

Refund requests associated with fraud, abuse, chargeback manipulation, or violations of the Terms of Service may be denied and may result in account suspension.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes become effective when published on this page.

8. Contact

Questions regarding refunds or cancellations may be submitted through the official Burkeonis website.
