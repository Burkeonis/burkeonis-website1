BEFORE YOU ENTER

DO THE WORK.
DON'T WEAPONIZE IT.

Do not use this page to build a prosecution against somebody else. The subject is your reaction, your defence and your next decision.

Understanding why a behaviour formed does not erase the damage it causes. Explanation is not immunity.

Do not force deep reflection while severely intoxicated, dangerously escalated or unable to stay oriented. Stabilize first.

This is an educational reflection tool, not diagnosis, treatment or emergency support.
