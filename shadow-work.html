SHADOW WORK ISN'T ABOUT BECOMING SOMEONE ELSE.

It's about becoming honest enough to meet the parts of yourself you've spent years avoiding.

Most of us don't fear the darkness. We fear what we'll find if we stop looking away.

WHAT IS A SHADOW?

Your shadow isn't evil.

It's made up of the emotions, memories, fears, habits, beliefs, and experiences you've pushed aside because they were painful, embarrassing, or too difficult to face.

Ignoring them doesn't make them disappear. It usually gives them more influence over your life.

WHY DO THE WORK?

Every pattern comes from somewhere.

Every reaction has a history.

Every defence once protected something.

Shadow work isn't about judging yourself. It's about understanding yourself clearly enough to choose differently.

THIS ISN'T EASY.

Some questions won't have comfortable answers.

Sometimes you'll realize you've been lying to yourself.

Sometimes you'll discover you've blamed people who didn't deserve it.

Sometimes you'll finally understand why you keep repeating the same cycle.

That's where the work begins.

WHERE TO START

Don't try to fix everything at once.

Pick one question.

Sit with it.

Write honestly.

Resist the urge to defend yourself.

Curiosity changes more than shame ever will.

THE GOAL

Shadow work isn't about becoming perfect.

It's about becoming someone who no longer has to hide from themselves.

Because you cannot change what you're unwilling to look at.
