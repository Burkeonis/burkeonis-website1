LOOK UNDER THE REACTION

Shadow work is not about fixing yourself — it’s about understanding the parts of you that react before you can think. These reactions are not random. They come from patterns, wounds, and emotional signatures that formed long before you realized they were shaping your life.

THE REACTION MAP

Every reaction has three layers:

  • THE SURFACE: what you feel immediately — anger, panic, shutdown, defensiveness.
  • THE TRIGGER: what the moment reminded you of — usually something old, not new.
  • THE SIGNATURE: the emotional pattern that repeats — the part of you that learned to protect itself.

When you understand these layers, you stop fighting the reaction and start understanding why it exists.

THE SHADOW WORK PROMPT

Use this whenever you feel overwhelmed, confused, or stuck in a loop. It helps you break down what is actually happening beneath the surface.

  1. What happened?
  2. What did I feel first?
  3. What did I assume?
  4. What did this moment remind me of?
  5. What pattern does this match?
  6. What part of me is trying to protect itself?

WHY THIS MATTERS

You cannot change what you refuse to see. Shadow work gives you the ability to understand your reactions instead of being controlled by them. When you know the pattern, you can break the cycle.
