THE PART
THAT TAKES OVER.

This was not written from a clean room or copied out of a textbook. It comes from addiction, loss, betrayal, rage, fractured identity, wrecked relationships, and the moments when everything hit at once. It is for finding the part of you that grabs the wheel before it wrecks something else.

NO EXCUSES. NO SELF-HATRED. NO BULLSHIT.

Other people can hurt you. What happened to you can be real and wrong. And your reaction can still become your responsibility. This file separates those truths so you can stop carrying what is not yours—and stop denying what is.
