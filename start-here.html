Why Burkeonis Exists

There was a time in my life when I hated looking in the mirror.

Not because of what I looked like.

Because I knew exactly who was staring back at me.

I saw someone carrying years of anger, regret, addiction, loss, and questions he didn't know how to answer. Every time life fell apart, I looked for another escape instead of another lesson. I spent years trying to outrun things that were never behind me. They were inside me.

The hardest truth I ever learned is that you can't run from yourself.

You take yourself everywhere.

When I finally stopped blaming everyone else and started asking myself the uncomfortable questions, something changed.

Not overnight.

Not all at once.

But enough to realize that the greatest battle I would ever fight wasn't against another person.

It was against the parts of myself I refused to face.

That's where Burkeonis was born.

Not as a brand.

Not as a business.

Not as a stage name.

Burkeonis became the version of me that refused to hide anymore.

I started writing music because I couldn't carry everything in silence. Every lyric became a confession. Every song became another piece of the story I had spent years trying to bury. Music gave a voice to emotions I didn't know how to explain any other way.

Then I asked myself a question that changed everything.

What if someone else is standing where I once stood?

What if they're searching for answers and all they're finding are empty motivational quotes, fake positivity, and people pretending to have perfect lives?

I wanted to build something different.

Something honest.

Something that could look people in the eye and say, "You're not broken beyond repair, but you're going to have to face yourself if you want your life to change."

That's why I create.

The music.

The apps.

The writing.

The ideas.

They're all pieces of the same mission.

Self Mirror exists because I wanted to build the conversation I wish I'd had years ago.

Tattoo Forge exists because I believe the symbols we carry on our bodies should tell the stories that changed our lives.

Every project on this website comes from the same place.

Real experience.

Real failure.

Real rebuilding.

I'm not interested in selling perfection because I don't have any.

I'm interested in building things with enough honesty that someone, somewhere, realizes they're not alone in what they're carrying.

If one song helps someone keep going for another day...

If one conversation helps someone recognize a destructive pattern...

If one app helps someone ask the question they've been avoiding...

Then every hour I've spent building this has been worth it.

This website isn't the destination.

It's the record of a man rebuilding his life in public.
