YOU DO NOT NEED
TO TAKE IN EVERYTHING.

Burkeonis is music, tools, writing, and unfinished work built around one mission: Know Thyself. Pick the door that feels closest to what you are carrying. The rest will still be here.

There was a time in my life when I hated looking in the mirror. Not because of what I looked like. Because I knew exactly who was staring back at me.

I saw someone carrying years of anger, regret, addiction, loss, and questions he didn’t know how to answer. Every time life fell apart, I looked for another escape instead of another lesson. I kept trying to outrun things that weren’t behind me. They were inside me.

The hardest truth I ever learned is that you can’t run from yourself. You take yourself everywhere.

When I finally stopped blaming everyone else and started asking myself the uncomfortable questions, something shifted. Not overnight. Not all at once. But enough to see that the biggest fight I’d ever face wasn’t against another person. It was against the parts of myself I kept avoiding.

That’s where Burkeonis came from. Not a brand. Not a business. Not a stage name. Burkeonis became the version of me that refused to hide anymore.

I started writing music because I couldn’t carry everything in silence. Every lyric felt like a confession. Every song was another piece of the story I’d been trying to bury. Music gave me a way to say things I didn’t know how to explain.

Then I asked myself something that changed everything. What if someone else is standing where I once stood?

What if they’re looking for real answers and all they’re finding is fake positivity and people pretending their lives are perfect?

I wanted to build something different. Something honest. Something real. Something that could look someone in the eye and say, “You’re not broken beyond repair. But you’re going to have to face yourself if you want your life to change.”

That’s why I create. The music. The apps. The writing. The ideas. They all come from the same place.

Self Mirror exists because I wanted the conversation I wish I had years ago. Tattoo Forge exists because I think the symbols we put on our bodies should actually mean something.

Every project on this site comes from real experience, real failure, and real rebuilding. I’m not here to sell perfection. I don’t have any. I’m here to build things honest enough that someone out there realizes they’re not alone in what they’re carrying.

If one song helps someone get through another day… If one conversation helps someone see a pattern they’ve been stuck in… If one app helps someone ask the question they’ve been avoiding…

Then every hour I’ve spent building this has been worth it.

This website isn’t the finish line. It’s the record of a man rebuilding his life in public.

Some days I’ll get it right. Some days I’ll mess it up. But I’ll keep creating.

Because if my story taught me anything, it’s this. The darkest parts of your life don’t have to be the end of your story. Sometimes they’re where your real story finally begins.

Welcome to Burkeonis. This is where I stopped running.
