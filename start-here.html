WHY THIS EXISTS

THE HARDEST PATTERN TO ESCAPE IS THE ONE YOU KEEP CARRYING.

For years I believed my life would change when something outside me changed. A different city. A different relationship. More money. Less pain. Another chance. Another escape.

Every restart still contained the same fears, anger, habits and unfinished damage.

Burkeonis came from finally looking at the distance between who I said I was and how I was actually living.

These tools are not here to flatter you, diagnose you or replace professional care. They are here to slow the moment down enough for honesty to become possible.
