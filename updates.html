THIS IS STILL BEING BUILT.

Burkeonis did not begin with a finished plan. It started with music, reflection, and the need to turn difficult experiences into something useful.

The direction has changed more than once. Some ideas became larger projects. Some were cut completely. Others are still being tested to see whether they deserve to exist.

This page shows what is working, what is being built, and what is still only an idea.

WHAT EXISTS NOW

  • Music Archive: Six Burkeonis tracks are available to play directly on the website, with individual song pages explaining what was behind them.
  • Self Mirror: A reflection tool built to question patterns, contradictions, avoidance, and the gap between words and actions.
  • RAD Mirror: A tool for breaking down intense reactions, triggers, emotional patterns, and what may be happening underneath them.
  • Shadow Work: The foundation for future worksheets, prompts, field notes, and practical reflection resources.
  • Burkeonis Website: The central home for the music, apps, tools, writing, and everything still being developed.

WHAT IS BEING BUILT

  • Tattoo Forge: A tattoo design tool combining personal meaning, runes, symbols, mythology, astrology, numerology, placement, and tattoo style.
  • Shadow Work Library: Printable worksheets and writing exercises focused on patterns, identity, relationships, responsibility, grief, addiction, fear, and personal change.
  • Field Notes: Short written reflections, observations, questions, and lessons collected while building Burkeonis.
  • App Development: Continued work on memory, privacy, accessibility, deeper reflection modes, and better ways to turn insight into action.
  • New Music: More Burkeonis releases, song pages, lyrics, and visual material.
  • The Burkeonis Book: A longer account of where this came from, what shaped it, and what it means to take responsibility for the person you became.

NOT EVERY IDEA WILL MAKE IT.

I have more ideas than time, money, or hands. That means some projects will be delayed, changed, combined, or dropped.

I am not interested in filling the website with empty promises. Something will only be released when it works well enough to be useful and honest enough to belong here.

The goal is not to build the largest collection of content. The goal is to build things that have a reason to exist.

WHAT COMES NEXT

The next stage is about finishing the foundation before adding more. That means improving the existing apps, releasing the first printable tools, continuing the music archive, and making the website easier to use.

Burkeonis will keep changing because I am still changing. The work stays visible here.
